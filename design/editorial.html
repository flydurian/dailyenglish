01 Phrasal verb

Feel free to drop by my office if you have any questions about the project.

Nuance

격식 없이 가볍게 들른다는 느낌. 짧은 방문이나 인사에 잘 어울립니다.

Example

Drop by anytime if you're around.

02 Phrasal verb

I decided to cut out sugar from my diet to improve my health.

건강을 위해 식단에서 설탕을 끊기로 했어요.

Nuance

습관이나 음식 등 무언가를 완전히 멈출 때 자연스럽게 씁니다.

Example

I'm trying to cut out late-night snacks.

03 Phrasal verb

There is a bad flu going around the office these days.

go around(병이) 돌다
Nuance

Example

A cold is going around the office.
